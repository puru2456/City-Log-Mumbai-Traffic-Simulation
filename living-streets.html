Module: Procedural Tarmac Synthesis

System Overview

Our algorithm doesn't just draw roads; it "grows" them. By feeding BMC (Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation) land-use records into a noise-based procedural generator, we simulate authentic urban wear-and-tear.

Logic Parameters

  • Density Map: Influences the "cracking" frequency of the road textures.
  • Flow Constraints: Dynamically shifts lane widths based on peak-hour bottleneck data.
  • Weathering: Simulates the impact of the Mumbai monsoon on tarmac degradation rates.
